The optional pool80 policy retains the exact isolated six-function launch binding
in pool80_launch.cu and the full swapAB derivative in coupled_kernel.cuh.
The launch file retains its original compilation-only comment as source history;
current activation is controlled by the documented model-owned opt-in policy.

coupled_kernel.cuh retains NVIDIA Apache-2.0 attribution and differs from the
original full vendor swapAB kernel only in its symbol and math-register request
(232 to 80). The derivative uses the existing unmodified vendor headers and N8
helper. All original license texts, NOTICE.txt and NOTICE-token8.txt apply.
The inherited full-kernel upstream source digest and packaged derivative hashes
are recorded in provenance.json. The original binding is MIT code.

This fixed policy does not establish integrated model quality, CUDA Graph state,
service performance or actual two-CTA residency. Those require separate evidence.
